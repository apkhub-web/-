Link Skip Tutorial (Vplink Ads)

Watch the full walkthrough, then follow the steps below.

  1. Tap Download. A Vplink page will open.
  2. Find the Skip or Continue button.
  3. If a timer is shown, wait 5–10 seconds. Do not click random ads.
  4. Close any popup with the X or Close button.
  5. On the final page, tap Download or Get Link.
  6. The APK will download from MediaFire.

APK Download & Install

  1. On the app details page, tap Download Now.
  2. Complete the Vplink steps from the tutorial above.
  3. Wait until the APK file finishes downloading.
  4. Open Settings, then Security, and turn on Install unknown apps for your browser or file manager.
  5. Open the downloaded APK and tap Install.
